Integrations

Keep your stack. Add the record.

AiVRIC connects your clouds, Microsoft 365, security tools, code and ticketing, and brings what they find into one workspace: one risk register, one set of evidence, one audit trail. Nothing gets ripped out.

Browse integrations → Ask about a connector

How it connects

Signals in. Work out.

01Read-only connectors.

Connect a cloud with read-only access and your first posture lands the same week.

02Your tools keep their jobs.

Findings from the scanners and platforms you already run come into the risk register, mapped to your frameworks, with the evidence kept.

03Work goes where teams are.

Findings and treatments go out to Jira, Teams and your SIEM, and their status comes back.

Running Client Vision for your clients? Each client's connectors pull only what that client allows, and the keys are sealed on arrival.

Questions

Before you connect anything.

Do we have to replace the tools we already run?

No. The scanners, platforms and ticketing you already run keep their jobs. AiVRIC brings what they find into one risk register, maps it to your frameworks and keeps the evidence.

Are the cloud connectors read-only?

Yes. Cloud connectors are read-only, and your first posture lands the same week you connect a cloud.

What do the labels mean?

Native: scanned directly by CloudSignals. Available now: an AiVRIC capability you can switch on today. Supported: an integration you can connect today. In build: being built now, not available yet. Planned: on the roadmap. Setup can vary by plan and deployment model.

Can findings go out to our ticketing and SIEM?

Yes. Jira takes findings as issues with two-way status sync, and Splunk and Microsoft Sentinel receive AiVRIC events. ServiceNow ITSM and Datadog are in build.

Do the integrations work in Client Vision?

Yes. In Client Vision each client’s connectors pull only what that client allows, and the keys are sealed on arrival.
