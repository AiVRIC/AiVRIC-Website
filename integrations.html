Microsoft 365 Integration

Turn Microsoft 365 into a risk operations workspace.

Most enterprise security and compliance teams already use Microsoft 365. AiVRIC is designed to work alongside it — routing risk workflows into Teams, storing evidence in SharePoint, delivering executive intelligence through Power BI, and governing identities via Purview and Entra. No new collaboration tools required.

End-to-end M365 risk workflow

1

Cloud Finding Detected

AiVRIC CloudSignals+RiskOps™ identifies a misconfiguration or compliance gap across AWS, Azure, or GCP in real time.

2

AI Risk Summary Generated

AiVRIC Vision AI creates a plain-language executive risk summary with severity context, remediation guidance, and control mapping.

3

Teams Review & Approval

The risk summary and proposed remediation action are routed to the responsible team via Microsoft Teams for human review and approval.

4

SharePoint Evidence Package

Upon approval, AiVRIC auto-generates a structured evidence package and stores it in the designated SharePoint library with audit metadata.

5

Power BI Executive Dashboard

Risk posture, compliance trends, and remediation progress are reflected in real time on the executive Power BI dashboard without manual data entry.

6

Remediation Tracked to Closure

The remediation action is tracked in ServiceNow or Jira with closure verified by AiVRIC — automatically updating the compliance record and evidence package.

Board-ready risk dashboards and compliance trend reporting delivered to leadership without manual build.

Purview

Data governance and classification alignment alongside cloud posture findings in a unified compliance view.

Entra ID

Identity posture and access review support aligned to cloud security baselines and compliance requirements.

Defender

Endpoint and identity security signals ingested into AiVRIC risk register for unified governance scoring.
