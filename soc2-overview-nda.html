How to request SOC 2 materials

AiVRIC maintains security posture documentation and audit evidence under NDA. Public summaries are available here; full documentation can be requested through the Trust Intake process.

  • Trust Service Criteria covered: Security (with supporting controls for availability and confidentiality).
  • Evidence includes: control descriptions, testing dates, auditor opinion, and scoped systems.
  • Request flow: initiate via Trust Intake or your account team; NDA required before release.
  • Supporting artifacts: policy summaries (encryption, access, logging), penetration testing summaries, and status badges surfaced in the Trust Center.

For procurement due diligence, we can also share a controls matrix and complete security questionnaires (CAIQ/SIG) under NDA.
