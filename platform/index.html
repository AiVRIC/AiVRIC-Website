The AiVRIC platform

One job each. Every decision on the record.

AiVRIC is a set of apps that each do one thing, and talk to each other through one signed contract. CloudSignals and AISignals sense. AIRE Agentic Mesh acts. Enterprise Vision and Client Vision show the program to the people who answer for it. Here is how it actually works.

How the apps fit → Ask an engineer

01 · How the apps fit

Four jobs, no overlap.

The platform's own rule book gives every app one sentence, and holds it to it. Observations stay with the app that saw them; decisions stay with the system of record.

Signal
Sense, normalize and publish. Nothing downstream of detection.
CloudSignals Available nowAISignals Available now
Decide
Where risks are treated, accepted or transferred, and who decided.
AiVRIC RiskOps In build
Act
Remediation planning and execution, under policy set by your people.
AIRE Agentic Mesh™ Available now
Show
Oversight, analysis and narrative, for everyone who answers for the program.
Enterprise Vision Available nowClient Vision Available now
No app reads another's database.

Each app keeps its own. They meet only through the published contract.

Every event is signed.

Findings, control changes, evidence and approvals travel as signed CloudEvents, and replays are rejected.

Written with the change.

An event is saved in the same transaction as the change it reports, so nothing is announced that didn't happen.

02 · The record

Isolated by the database. Checked by the chain.

01The database keeps tenants apart.

Row-level security in every app: a workspace sees only its own rows, with no access by default. The application can't widen it.

02One record per weakness.

When several tools report the same problem, Enterprise Vision keeps one record, with each source's own words beside the normalized ones.

03A trail that checks itself.

The audit trail is append-only and hash-chained. Anyone with audit rights can re-walk the chain and see that nothing was changed.

How Vision reads it. Enterprise Vision and Client Vision read CloudSignals through its MCP server, on a schedule you set (every six hours, daily or weekly), and write governance decisions back to it the same way.

03 · AI that shows its work

It explains the record. A person signs off.

The AI in AiVRIC doesn't free-associate. It works from the record in front of it, and what it writes is a draft until someone approves it.

AiVA, in both Vision apps

Available now
  • Explains a finding, control, vendor, risk or incident from that record's own facts
  • Won't save an answer that names something the record doesn't hold
  • Writes narratives and suggestions as drafts a person approves
  • Runs on Anthropic, through AiVRIC's key or your workspace's own
  • Prompts aren't stored; the explanation or draft is

Chat in CloudSignals

Available now
  • Questions about your posture, answered from your scans
  • Your choice of model provider: Anthropic, OpenAI, Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint
  • Your own provider keys
  • Personal data scrubbed before anything reaches the model

Metered, not surprising. Every AI call is counted against your organization's monthly allowance. You're warned at 80% and it stops at 100%, so a busy month can't run up a bill nobody saw.

04 · Governed remediation · AIRE Agentic Mesh

Fixes on a ladder you control.

AIRE runs remediation from a catalog of known actions, each with its own rollback. You decide how far it may go on its own, per playbook and per environment. An unknown environment counts as production.

L0ObserveIt watches and reports. Nothing changes.
L1RecommendIt proposes the fix; people do it.
L2Approve to executeIt acts once a second person approves.
L3Auto, with guardrailsIt acts inside the guardrails you set.
L4Autonomous within budgetIt acts on its own, up to a budget.

Hard floorsSome actions never go above L1, whatever the setting: deleting data or keys, break-glass identity changes, and turning off logging or monitoring.

Two people, not one.

The person who approves is never the person who asked, and the approval is signed.

Small first, then wider.

Changes land in canary waves, starting with a single target, and roll back if health checks regress.

Verified, then sealed.

A separate read-only identity checks the result, and every case ends in a signed, hash-chained evidence record.

Where it starts. The catalog covers AWS today. An AI model can advise on a plan, but only advises, and that's switched off by default.

05 · AI security · AISignals

See your AI. Then test it.

AISignals watches the language models you run in production and tests them against the ways AI goes wrong, with the evidence kept for your reviewers.

Observability

  • Traces of every model call, with cost and latency
  • Evaluations, including hallucination, scored by a model acting as judge
  • Built on the open-source Langfuse engine

31 AI security checks

  • Across prompts, outputs, RAG, agents and tools, MCP servers, privacy and the supply chain
  • Run on your own runner, so your traffic stays with you
  • Evidence redacted by default
  • Reports mapped to the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act, and designed to support ISO/IEC 42001 programs

06 · How it runs

Sized to your estate, one scan at a time.

01Every scan, its own job.

Each scan runs as its own Kubernetes job, with its own pod and an identity that can't touch the cluster. Memory and CPU are picked from five sizes, by how big your estate was last time.

02Scales with demand.

The service behind the apps adds and removes copies of itself as load changes.

03Open at the core.

The CloudSignals scanner is built on open-source Prowler, extended by AiVRIC with attack-surface checks of its own.

5public clouds: AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle Cloud and Alibaba Cloud
12kinds of source you can connect, from clouds to Kubernetes, Microsoft 365 and GitHub
1,000+posture checks across those sources
40+compliance frameworks mapped

Counted from the scanner's code, November 2026.

07 · Where data lives

On Google Cloud, in the US.

01Hosted for you.

The hosted service runs on Google Cloud in the United States.

02Your own environment, on request.

A namespace and a database of your own, for teams whose review asks for it. In Client Vision, every provider gets a database instance of its own.

03Requests on the record.

Requests to keep data in a particular place, or to purge a tenant, run through a logged workflow, not an email thread.
