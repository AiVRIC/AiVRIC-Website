Privacy & data handling

Your data lives where you decide.

AiVRIC is hosted on Google Cloud in the US. Pick how your workspace runs and see where the data lives, who can touch it, and which subprocessors apply.

AI · 01Not used for training.

Customer data isn't used to train AiVRIC or third-party AI models by default. It takes your explicit, written opt-in.

AI · 02Prompts aren't stored.

The apps keep what the AI wrote, the explanation or the draft, and a person approves every draft. What was sent to the model isn't kept.

AI · 03Your key, if you want it.

AI features run on Anthropic. A workspace can use its own Anthropic key; otherwise it runs on AiVRIC's, with Anthropic listed below as a subprocessor.

Questions

What security reviews ask first.

Does AiVRIC have a SOC 2 report?

SOC 2 Type II, covering Security and Availability, is on our roadmap. Today we can share an ISO/IEC 27001 alignment summary (with the control mapping under NDA), a NIST CSF mapping, and a penetration test executive summary under NDA. Ask through Trust Intake about SOC 2 timing.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Customer data isn’t used to train AiVRIC or third-party AI models by default; it takes your explicit, written opt-in. AI features run on Anthropic, through AiVRIC’s key or your workspace’s own, and prompts aren’t stored.

Where is our data stored?

On Google Cloud in the US. Each workspace is isolated in the database by row-level security; a dedicated namespace and database is available on request, and in Client Vision each provider gets its own database.

Can you complete our security questionnaire?

Yes. We support CAIQ, SIG Lite and Full, and custom questionnaires through Trust Intake. Include your deadline and we’ll route it to the right owner.

How do we get documents that are under NDA?

Add them to a Trust Intake request on this page, or email trust@aivric.com. We verify the NDA before anything confidential goes out.

How do I report a vulnerability?

Email security@aivric.com with reproduction steps and the impact you observed, without sensitive data in the first message. We acknowledge receipt, coordinate the fix with you, and ask that you coordinate with us before public disclosure.
