Key Capabilities

Aligned to enterprise requirements

Four foundational capabilities that unify security intelligence and governance across complex multi-business-unit environments.

Multi-tenant Governance

Separate data, policies, and visibility by business unit. Independent compliance postures with centralized oversight and cross-tenant rollup reporting.

Custom Analytics

Advanced reporting and business intelligence on top of your security and compliance data. Build dashboards that map to your organizational risk taxonomy.

Localization Controls

Policy-based data residency for multi-region deployments. Enforce data sovereignty requirements with configurable retention, export, and purge policies.

Executive Reporting

Board-ready risk intelligence with AI-generated summaries, trend analysis, and audit-ready evidence packages formatted for leadership reviews.

Security & Compliance

Frameworks supported

Mapped controls, evidence, and reporting for the enterprise compliance requirements your organization faces most.

SOC 2 (Security, Availability, Confidentiality)
Requirement: Maintain continuous monitoring of security controls and retain audit evidence.
  • Centralizes control evidence and logs for auditors.
  • Tracks remediation activity with approvals and timestamps.
  • Provides executive-ready compliance reporting.
ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security Management)
Requirement: Establish risk treatment controls and demonstrate ongoing effectiveness.
  • Maps findings to control objectives and risk registers.
  • Documents remediation and verification workflows.
  • Maintains evidence for continuous assurance.
PCI-DSS (Payment Card Security)
Requirement: Monitor systems, detect misconfigurations, and document remediation actions.
  • Flags posture gaps and policy violations in real time.
  • Produces audit-ready reports for assessors.
  • Tracks remediation with accountability.
CMMC (Federal Contractor Readiness)
Requirement: Implement security practices and keep verifiable evidence of performance.
  • Aligns controls to maturity requirements.
  • Maintains POA&Ms and exception documentation.
  • Supports evidence collection for audits.

Enterprise FAQs

Answers for security, compliance & procurement teams

Common questions from enterprise buyers evaluating Vision Enterprise for their security program.

What deployment models are available?

SaaS, customer-hosted SaaS, or private deployment options with shared controls and audit artifacts. Review the Trust Center.

How does data residency and retention work?

Regional data residency, configurable retention, and export/purge policies to meet your governance requirements. Review the Trust Center.

What integrations are supported?

Native connectors and APIs for SIEM/SOAR, ticketing systems (Jira, ServiceNow), and CI/CD workflows. Review the Trust Center.

Does it support SSO, SCIM, and RBAC?

SSO and SCIM provisioning with granular RBAC for least-privilege access across business units. Review the Trust Center.

What evidence and audit support is included?

Evidence packages, control mappings, and audit-ready reporting for assessors — formatted for SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, and CMMC. Review the Trust Center.

What are the support and SLA options?

Business and enterprise support tiers with response-time SLAs, dedicated customer success, and escalation paths. Review the Trust Center.
