Preparedness

Response process and roles

AiVRIC follows the Incident Response Operations standard (IRO) with defined severities, on-call rotations, and customer notification steps. The playbook is tested through tabletop and functional exercises.

  • Severity model with triage timelines; security engineering owns containment and eradication.
  • Customer notification criteria and channels defined for data-impacting events.
  • Forensic logging retained per Logging & Monitoring; evidence preserved for post-incident review.
  • Post-incident review with documented lessons learned and tracked remediation actions.

The Trust Center highlights current readiness posture; detailed runbooks are available to customers under NDA upon request.
