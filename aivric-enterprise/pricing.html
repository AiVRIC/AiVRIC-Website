Pricing FAQs

Straight answers to common procurement and third-party security review attestation questionnaires.

What counts as a Workload Unit (WU)?

A WU is a billable compute-like workload such as a VM, container workload, or serverless application. You can optionally extend WU counting to include certain managed services based on your roadmap and customer demand.

What is an “Instance” and when do I need more than one?

An Instance is a separated deployment boundary used to segregate data, access, and operational controls across business units, environments (dev/test/prod), subsidiaries, or regulatory partitions. Enterprise customers commonly adopt multi-instance for assurance.

How does AiVRIC compete with open source options?

Baseline scanning is only one component of operational maturity. AiVRIC differentiates through repeatable workflows, evidence and audit outputs, governance, automation, and multi-instance operations—capabilities that reduce time-to-assurance and total effort.

Is BYO-AI supported?

Yes. You can bring your own AI API key for AI-assisted triage and reporting. If preferred, Managed AI can be purchased as an add-on with defined usage limits.

Do you offer multi-year discounts?

Yes. Multi-year terms and volume tiers are available for qualified customers. A common pattern is 10–15% off for 2-year and 15–25% off for 3-year agreements, with guardrails to protect long-term margin.

Can we start with Windows and later move to customer-hosted SaaS?

Yes. Many customers begin with Windows for rapid posture assessment, then transition to customer-hosted SaaS for continuous operations. Your team can structure a migration plan and map pricing to preserve value and continuity.
