AiVRIC Fabric data localization controls by policy and region.

The AiVRIC Fabric enforces residency, sovereignty, and retention requirements across your security and AI data. Keep sensitive data where it belongs without sacrificing visibility.

Meeting global data residency obligations

Regulations demand strict controls over where data is stored and processed. AiVRIC enables compliant operations for global enterprises and regulated industries.

  • Regulatory restrictions on cross-border data movement.
  • Need for tenant isolation and sovereign controls.
  • Audit expectations for data handling and retention.

Key capabilities

The AiVRIC Fabric delivers purpose-built capabilities so Data Localization supports agentic security, GRC, and AI trust workflows.

Regional data stores

Keep telemetry and evidence in approved regions.

Tenant isolation

Segregate data and workflows by business unit or client.

Key ownership

Support customer-managed encryption keys and policies.

Redaction controls

Mask sensitive fields across chat, dashboards, and reports.

How localization is enforced

  • Define residency policies by region, tenant, or data type.
  • Route ingestion and processing to approved environments.
  • Audit and report on compliance with localization controls.

Outcomes you can expect

  • Meet regulatory requirements without sacrificing insight.
  • Reduce risk of cross-border data violations.
  • Deliver trust to customers and auditors.

Deployment options

  • Supports regional SaaS, customer-hosted SaaS, or on-prem.
  • Compatible with sovereign cloud and private deployments.
  • Configurable retention and deletion policies.
