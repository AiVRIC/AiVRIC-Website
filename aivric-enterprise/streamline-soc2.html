Accelerate SOC 2 Readiness with Autonomous Evidence and Continuous Controls

Modern organizations cannot afford long, manual SOC 2 cycles. AiVRIC transforms SOC 2 readiness into a continuous, automated process?reducing audit timelines, eliminating documentation gaps, and equipping executives and auditors with accurate, real-time insights.

By combining cloud security posture management, continuous controls monitoring, automated evidence collection, and AI-driven narratives, AiVRIC helps you move from reactive compliance work to a predictable, efficient, and scalable SOC 2 operating model.

The SOC 2 Challenge

Achieving and maintaining SOC 2 compliance requires more than a set of policies. It demands consistent and defensible proof that your security controls are designed and operating effectively.

  • Complex, multi-cloud and SaaS environments
  • Manual, time-consuming evidence collection
  • Inconsistent or incomplete documentation
  • Limited visibility for executives and auditors

How AiVRIC Automates SOC 2 Compliance

AiVRIC eliminates the heavy lift of compliance operations by automating the entire chain from control to evidence to audit narrative. The platform continuously evaluates your posture against SOC 2 expectations and prepares evidence bundles that are ready for auditor review.

Continuous Controls Monitoring

AiVRIC continuously evaluates cloud, identity, logging, and network configurations against SOC 2 control expectations, providing early warning for drift and misconfigurations before they become audit findings.

Automated Evidence Collection

Evidence is captured directly from your systems?configurations, logs, posture checks, and asset inventories?so your team spends less time gathering screenshots and more time improving security.

AI-Generated Narratives

Lighthouse AI transforms posture and control data into clear, consistent descriptions of how your controls are designed and operated, simplifying Section III narrative development and auditor communication.

Risk Register Intelligence

Findings from AiVRIC are automatically mapped to your risk register and aligned with business impact, ensuring that SOC 2 risks are evaluated, prioritized, and tracked to closure.

Outcomes You Can Expect

With AiVRIC, organizations typically experience:

  • Up to 70% reduction in SOC 2 audit preparation time
  • Consistent and defensible evidence for controls across all in-scope systems
  • Accelerated readiness for both Type 1 and Type 2 audits
  • Proactive remediation of posture gaps before auditors arrive
  • Improved collaboration between security, engineering, and compliance teams
