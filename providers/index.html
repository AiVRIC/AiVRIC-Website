    True multi-tenancy

    Every client in its own workspace.
    Every entry on their record.

    The first thing a client asks its provider: who can get into our program, and would we know?

    • Sealed from each other.There is no data path between client tenants. Each client’s program lives in its own workspace.
    • Provider authority, not membership.You enter a client’s workspace from your console, and a band says so on every screen until you exit.
    • On the client’s own audit trail.Every entry lands in that client’s append-only audit trail.
    • Only what each client allows.Connectors pull from what each client allows. Nothing outside the client’s scope is requested, stored or shown, and keys are sealed on arrival.
