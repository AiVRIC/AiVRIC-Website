Security

Core control themes

Our security posture is organized around practical, verifiable controls: identity and access, secure configuration, logging and monitoring, vulnerability management, and resilient operations. The exact implementation depends on deployment model and customer requirements.

Identity & Access

Role-based access, least privilege, and strong authentication options.

  • Admin and user roles separated; permissions scoped by function.
  • Support for enterprise authentication patterns (e.g., SSO/SAML) where applicable.
  • Auditability for access and key administrative actions.

Platform Security

Secure defaults, configuration hardening, and defensible guardrails.

  • Secure configuration baselines and hardened deployment guidance.
  • Change control practices supporting consistent and repeatable releases.
  • Secrets and sensitive values protected and minimized by design.

Monitoring & Response

Operational telemetry designed to support detection and investigation.

  • Event and activity logging aligned to operational needs.
  • Alerting patterns for anomalies and critical platform events.
  • Incident handling procedures with internal escalation and communication pathways.

Compliance

Program alignment & evidence

AiVRIC supports compliance operations by producing structured evidence, mappings, and reporting outputs. Below are common frameworks customers align to. Confirm exact scope and applicability with your security review, particularly for regulated environments and customer-hosted deployments.

Supported framework alignments

Commonly used baselines and governance frameworks in customer environments.

  • SOC 2 (AICPA Trust Services Criteria) — evidence-oriented outputs for readiness and audit support.
  • ISO/IEC 27001 — control mapping support for ISMS alignment and continuous posture tracking.
  • NIST CSF — governance and risk posture communication for leadership reporting.
  • CIS Benchmarks — configuration posture support where applicable to environment scope.

Evidence-first workflows

Designed to reduce time-to-assurance by keeping documentation and outputs audit-ready.

  • Executive summaries for leadership, plus exportable evidence artifacts.
  • Repeatable reporting cadence: point-in-time and trend-based posture tracking.
  • Clear traceability between checks, findings, and mapped framework references.
  • Release-to-release change visibility for mappings and checks (see FAQ).

Privacy & data handling

Minimize • control • segregate

Data handling depends on your deployment model. AiVRIC is designed to minimize sensitive data exposure and support customer control over storage and access. For demos, use non-sensitive data wherever possible.

Data minimization

Collect and retain only what is needed to deliver outcomes.

  • Prefer metadata and configuration posture over content collection.
  • Support redaction and safe handling patterns for reports and exports.

Customer control

Support customer ownership and governance expectations.

  • Deployment options structured for customer data control and segregation.
  • Access controlled by customer-managed identities where applicable.

Retention & export

Operational transparency and portability.

  • Exportable findings and summaries to support governance workflows.
  • Retention options guided by customer policy and engagement scope.

Reliability & operations

Resilience by design

Reliability is built through repeatable operations: secure updates, monitored services, and disciplined change management. For production deployments, we recommend validating the target operating model (support boundaries, SLAs, and ownership).

Change management

Release discipline designed to support consistent outcomes and fewer surprises.

  • Documented changes to checks, mappings, and major workflows.
  • Versioned release notes and guidance for upgrade planning.

Service readiness

Operational visibility and stability are treated as first-class requirements.

  • Monitoring aligned to platform health and critical events.
  • Support pathways and escalation processes defined per customer needs.

FAQ

Security & procurement

Replace these answers with your finalized position statements. Keep responses crisp and verifiable—security teams want clarity on boundaries, ownership, and how you handle change.

How does AiVRIC define “current” framework support?

“Current” typically means the latest stable, published version of a framework or benchmark as recognized by its issuing body. AiVRIC may also treat a prior version as “current” for a limited overlap period to support customer transition. See the Framework Support Policy for specifics.

How do you communicate changes to checks and mappings release-to-release?

We recommend publishing versioned release notes for check logic changes, mapping updates, and material workflow changes. For enterprise customers, provide an optional change log package (diff summary) to support audit and risk review.

What should we use in the demo environment?

Use non-sensitive data whenever possible (sample repositories, test subscriptions, or non-production assets). The demo environment is for evaluation and should not be used for production workloads.

Do you support security questionnaires and procurement reviews?

Yes. Use “Request Security Review” to route your questionnaire and scope details. We will align responses to your deployment model and requirements (identity, segregation, data handling, and compliance targets).
