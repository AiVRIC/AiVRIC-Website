Continuous and Comprehensive Risk Visibility

AiVRIC's Security & Risk Assessment service provides a holistic and continuous evaluation of your organization's security posture. Our platform empowers you to proactively identify, analyze, and manage risks across your entire digital landscape. By offering a unified view of your assets and their associated vulnerabilities, we enable you to prioritize threats and make informed decisions to safeguard your business.

We go beyond traditional periodic assessments by integrating continuous monitoring and automated scanning. This ensures that you have real-time visibility into your security posture, allowing for the immediate detection of new and emerging threats. Our goal is to provide you with the comprehensive insights needed to maintain a robust and resilient security framework.

Key Components

Our Security & Risk Assessment is built on a foundation of continuous discovery, in-depth analysis, and actionable intelligence to provide a complete picture of your risk landscape.

  • Automated Attack Surface Scanning
  • Continuous Vulnerability Assessment
  • Threat-Based Risk Prioritization
  • Comprehensive Reporting & Analytics

Our Assessment Methodology

AiVRIC employs a multi-faceted approach to risk assessment, ensuring that all potential security gaps are identified and evaluated in the context of your business.

Asset & Network Discovery

We begin by mapping your entire digital footprint, including known and unknown assets, to ensure no part of your attack surface is left unmonitored.

Contextual Risk Analysis

Our platform analyzes vulnerabilities in the context of asset criticality and threat intelligence, providing a prioritized list of risks that matter most to your organization.

With AiVRIC's continuous risk assessment, you can confidently manage your security posture, respond to threats more effectively, and demonstrate compliance with greater ease.
