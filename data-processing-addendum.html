Customer data processing

Summary of AiVRIC DPA terms

The AiVRIC Data Processing Addendum (DPA) incorporates GDPR/CCPA-aligned commitments for customers who process personal data through AiVRIC. It references the Privacy Policy and Trust Center controls for encryption, access, logging, retention, and subprocessors.

  • Roles: AiVRIC acts as a processor; customers remain controllers of their data.
  • Security: controls summarized in Trust Center and related policies (encryption, access control, incident response).
  • Subprocessors: listed in the Subprocessor List with notice/objection mechanisms where applicable.
  • Data subject rights: handled per the Privacy Policy with defined response SLAs.
  • Transfers: supports SCCs/appropriate safeguards for cross-border data transfers.

The full DPA is available upon request via Trust Intake; this page provides the public summary and linkage to the governing privacy terms.
