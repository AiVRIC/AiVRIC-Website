About AiVRIC

Security programs, built as systems.

Security and compliance rarely fail for lack of intent or tools. They fail because they aren't designed as systems. AiVRIC builds the system: two apps, one for your company and one for the providers who serve clients, with every finding, decision and fix on the record.

How we build

The rules we hold our own code to.

01One job each.

Every app does one thing and talks to the others through one signed contract. No app reads another's database.

02The database keeps tenants apart.

Row-level security in every app, with no access by default. The application can't widen it.

03People approve; the record proves.

AI drafts, people sign off, and the audit trail is hash-chained so anyone with audit rights can check it.

Where we come from

Built by practitioners.

AiVRIC was founded by people who ran security and compliance programs where failure was expensive: pharmaceutical manufacturing under FDA scrutiny, global banking, and private equity portfolios. It grew out of 3HUE Security, a security advisory firm. The pattern was the same everywhere: controls passed audits now and then, governance existed on paper, and the evidence lived in fragments.
