Automate and Simplify Your Compliance Journey

AiVRIC's Compliance Management solution streamlines and automates adherence to a wide range of security and privacy frameworks, including PCI DSS, SOC 2, CMMC L2, and ISO 27001. Our platform is designed to simplify the complexities of compliance, enabling your organization to achieve and maintain its required certifications with less effort and greater accuracy.

By leveraging automation, we help you eliminate the manual, time-consuming tasks associated with evidence collection, control mapping, and audit preparation. Our continuous monitoring capabilities ensure that your compliance posture is always up-to-date, providing real-time visibility and immediate alerts on any deviations. This proactive approach transforms compliance from a periodic, stressful event into a seamless, ongoing business process.

Core Capabilities

Our Compliance Management platform is engineered to provide a comprehensive, end-to-end solution for all your compliance needs, from initial assessment to final audit.

  • Automated Control Mapping & Evidence Collection
  • Continuous Compliance Monitoring
  • Audit-Ready Reporting & Dashboards
  • Custom Framework & Policy Support

Our Compliance Process

AiVRIC follows a structured and transparent process to ensure that your organization can navigate the complexities of compliance with confidence and clarity.

Framework Implementation

We start by mapping your existing controls to your chosen frameworks, identifying gaps and providing clear guidance on how to address them.

Evidence Automation & Management

Our platform automatically collects and securely stores tamper-evident data, creating a complete and reliable audit trail that simplifies auditor requests.

With AiVRIC, you can be confident that you are always prepared for your next audit. Our streamlined process and automated evidence collection save you time, reduce stress, and ensure a successful outcome.
