Program brief

AiVRIC Insider Early Access Program

Secure Remediation. Continuous Visibility. Real Outcomes.

Limited public debut as customer-hosted SaaS. AiVRIC Insider Early Access is designed for organizations that require real risk reduction, not experimentation.
Ask for a confirmation call

Why Insider Early Access exists

Most security platforms are introduced as tools. AiVRIC is being introduced as a system.

Security and compliance fail not because teams do not care or tools do not exist, they fail because they were never designed to operate continuously.

This program delivers a real-world, production-grade experience of continuous, system-driven security in your environment.

What this program is (and is not)

  • This program is:
  • A founder-led early access program.
  • A real production deployment of AiVRIC in your environment.
  • Focused on execution, remediation, and operational clarity.
  • Designed to produce defensible, audit-ready outcomes.
  • This program is not:
  • A beta release.
  • A software trial.
  • A demo environment.
  • A compliance checklist exercise.

What Insider Early Access includes

1. Cyber risk assessment (AiVRIC-powered)

  • Risk visibility across cloud, identity, configuration, and control layers.
  • Signal-driven findings, not raw scanner output.
  • Business-aligned risk prioritization.

2. Ongoing risk visibility

  • Customer-hosted AiVRIC CloudInsights SaaS access for 120 days.
  • Continuous insight after remediation.
  • No forced platform commitment beyond the program.

Common use cases

  • SOC 2 posture validation and audit readiness.
  • PCI-DSS readiness and cloud control alignment.
  • Annual cloud vulnerability and configuration risk assessments.
  • Board-driven risk reduction initiatives.
  • Pre- or post-incident remediation and architecture hardening.

Program structure & pricing

Duration

120-day fixed-term engagement.

Delivery

Self-hosted and managed, or partner-led delivery and support.

Pricing

Limited no-cost access, no restrictions.

Commitment

No long-term contract required. Insider pricing is available only during this early access phase.

Who this is for

  • Regulated SaaS and cloud-native organizations.
  • Security leaders under audit or board pressure.
  • Teams that need remediation, not more findings.
  • Organizations evaluating AiVRIC in real operating conditions.
