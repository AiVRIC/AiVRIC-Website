Questions

Before you pick a plan.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. CloudSignals Free connects one cloud account and runs posture scans with findings in the workspace, within the plan’s limits. No credit card and no sales call.

What happens when the Growth Trial ends?

It becomes Premium unless you cancel before the trial ends. Starting the trial takes a payment method.

Where are the figures?

Current figures are on the CloudSignals plans page. For Enterprise and White-label Provider, we walk you through pricing on the call.

Can we run it self-hosted or air-gapped?

Yes, on Enterprise. Enterprise Vision runs SaaS, self-hosted or air-gapped; talk to us about which fits.

We’re an MSP or MSSP. Which plan is ours?

White-label Provider, in Client Vision: every client on one console in your own brand. You can buy direct or join the Service Delivery Partner Program.

Do AISignals, AiVRIC RiskOps and AIRE Agentic Mesh cost extra?

They’re in build and not sold yet. When they’re ready they arrive in the app you already run, and we’ll set out how they’re packaged then.
