About AiVRIC Technologies

Agentic security, GRC, and AI evaluations in one platform.

AiVRIC Technologies is a software development company building the AiVRIC Platform for agentic enterprise security. We unify CloudSignals, AI Signals™, RogueAgent ASPM™, and Vision to serve cybersecurity, GRC management, and AI trust teams.

The platform aggregates telemetry from structured and unstructured sources into a centralized data lake, transforming it into a private GenAI datastore that powers agentic insights and AI-assisted workflows.

Unified intelligence model

Correlate defense and offense telemetry, AI evaluations, and compliance data in one system of record.

Private GenAI readiness
