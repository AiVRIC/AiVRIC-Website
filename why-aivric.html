The AiVRIC difference

Security and compliance, run as one program.

Most organizations run security and compliance as two separate, mostly manual operations, each with its own tools and its own version of the truth. AiVRIC puts the findings, the decisions and the evidence in one program, with every step on the record.

The four principles → Book a walkthrough

The problem

More tools, less of an answer.

Cloud posture tools, compliance platforms, evidence trackers, pen-test reports and AI monitoring each keep their own findings. Teams spend their time correlating instead of acting, auditors wait for evidence, and leadership can't get a straight answer to "how exposed are we?"

Four principles

What every part of AiVRIC is held to.

01

One record per weakness

When several tools report the same problem, Enterprise Vision keeps one record of it, with each source's own words beside the normalized ones. You fix it once.

02

Decisions on the record

Risk appetite, accepted risks, exceptions and approvals are written down where the auditor will look, on an append-only, hash-chained audit trail that checks itself.

03

AI that shows its work

The assistant explains a record from that record's own facts, won't save an answer that names something the record doesn't hold, and writes drafts a person approves.

04

Fixes under governance

AIRE Agentic Mesh remediates on a ladder you set, with a second person's signed approval, a separate check of the result, and sealed evidence for every case.
